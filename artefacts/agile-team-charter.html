Agile Team Charter

Artefact 1 of 5 · Evidence + Reflection (20 marks)

Purpose

The GoShuttle team exists to deliver a reliable, intuitive campus shuttle platform that helps CPUT students and staff move between campuses with confidence. We succeed when users can plan trips, track buses, and access their digital pass without friction.

Core Values

  • User first — every decision is judged by whether it helps the commuter.
  • Transparency — work is visible on the Trello board; blockers are raised early.
  • Quality over speed — no feature ships without basic testing and a clean UI.
  • Respect and honesty — feedback is direct but constructive.

Working Agreements

AgreementDetail
Daily check-in10 minutes, posted in the team channel by 09:00.
Definition of DoneCode reviewed, tested locally, committed with a clear message, card moved to "Done".
Sprint lengthOne week (aligned to the assessment window: 20–27 Oct 2026).
Board disciplineEvery task lives on Trello; no work is "invisible".
Commit hygieneOne logical change per commit; reference the card in the message.

Operational Boundaries

  • Scope is limited to frontend features listed in the product backlog; backend work is out of scope this iteration.
  • No third-party UI libraries — styling uses plain CSS tokens to keep the bundle small.
  • All authentication copy must require a CPUT email and an 8+ character password.
  • Changes affecting the shared header/footer require a second review.

Evidence

This charter is referenced in the Trello board description and guides prioritisation in the Product Backlog.
